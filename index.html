The Label

Built in the underground.

Decibel Distribution started as a bedroom operation pushing tapes out of a trunk. Today we're a full-service independent label and distro house — mixing, mastering, pressing, and getting your record onto every major streaming platform — without asking you to sand off what makes it yours.

01 / Distribution

Global Reach

Full delivery to Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud, YouTube, TikTok, and 150+ platforms worldwide.

02 / Pressing

Vinyl & Tape

Limited-run vinyl and cassette pressings for artists who want something you can actually hold.

03 / A&R

Real Support

Studio time, mixing/mastering, cover art, and playlist pushes for every artist on the roster.
